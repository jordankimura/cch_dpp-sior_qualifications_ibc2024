Special Inspector Lookup

Who may perform each class of special inspection, and what they must hold. Draft, built on the 2024 IBC as amended by the Hawaii State Building Code and ROH 16-1.1 — not yet adopted. Certification requirements reproduced from the DPP matrix and unedited.

Reading the requirements. “+” means all of. “or” means any one of. “—” means not permitted by that route. Sub-letters such as Other(b) point to the second option listed in that certification cell.

Draft status. Honolulu currently adopts the Hawaii State Building Code of 20 April 2021, which adopts the 2018 IBC. This lookup reflects a proposed move to the 2024 edition and is not an adopted requirement. Two City sections collide with base-code numbers under the 2024 edition and are awaiting renumbering.
