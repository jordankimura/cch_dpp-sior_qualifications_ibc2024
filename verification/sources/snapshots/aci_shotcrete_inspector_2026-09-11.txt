Certification Programs
ACI offers over 30 certification programs designed to form a minimum qualification for personnel employed within the concrete construction industry. Certification examinations and optional training courses are conducted through an international network of over 135 sponsoring groups. Since 1983, ACI has administered exams to over 900,000 individuals, with over 120,000 certifications currently maintained by ACI.
