NFCA Education Programs & Exams
Welcome to NFCA’s Education & Fireproofing Exams
The NFCA Fireproofing Education and Exams for NFCA Contractor Accreditation Program (CAP) and UL Designated Responsible Individuals (DRI’s) and Inspectors is designed to provide a benchmark level of knowledge to participants. Covering Sprayed Fire-Resistive Materials and or Intumescent Fire-Resistive Materials, the NFCA covers fireproofing code requirements, test standards, application standards, tested and listed system designs, and much more. Both courses and exams can be taken during the same session.
