## Fix before this goes to DPP

These are errors in the workbook, ranked by how much damage they'd do.

### 1. "ICC has no exam 49" is false — CONTRADICTED
ICC's special inspector page lists **Category 49, Reinforced Concrete Special Inspector** (Category 47 plus ACI Grade I). ICC **92 Prestressed Concrete requires Category 49.** The DPP entry "Pre-stressed Concrete (92) & Reinforced Concrete (49)" on SI-015 is consistent with ICC's own prerequisite chain.
- **Wrong in:** Credential check row 4 (status ERROR), Flags SI-015, Decision 10
- **Source:** iccsafe.org/professional-development/certifications-and-testing/special-inspector-exams/ (retrieved 2026-09-11)

### 2. "EC is not a current designation" is false — CONTRADICTED
ICC names the certification **"EC Soils"**. ECC and ECP are the two exam modules that make it up. "Soils (EC)" is the current certification name. IAS AC291 (effective 2024-01-01) also uses "Soil Special Inspector (EC)".
- **Wrong in:** Credential check row 7 (status ERROR)

### 3. "86M" and "Only 86 is unchanged" contradict each other, and the ICC page supports neither
ICC lists **86 Spray-applied Fireproofing** as the GR module plus a combined Codes and Plans exam numbered 86. No "86M" appears.
- **Wrong in:** Credential check row 10 ("86M"), Decision 10 ("Only 86 is unchanged")

### 4. Wrong column letters — DPP would look at the wrong cells
In Matrix 2024, **H = DPOR/EOR, I = Employee under DPOR, J = 3rd party, K = Registered SI, L–P = ICC/ACI/NICET/AWS/Other.**
The welding and high-strength-bolting exception in ROH amend. (111) restricts the DPOR and their personnel. That's **columns H and I**, not J and K.
- Flags G7 and G16, Decisions C5: "columns J and K" → **H and I**
- Decisions B12: "column N" (ICC) → **L**
- Change Log C6 and B18: "columns N through R" → **L through P**
- Drop-down A33: still gives the validation ranges as B1:B23 / B1:B26. They are now **B1:B29**.

### 5. Firestop exams are inspector qualifications — CONTRADICTED
The workbook treats "UL Firestop Exam, & Factory Mutual Firestop Exam" (SI-032) as contractor accreditation. It confuses the **FM 4991 contractor program** with the **FM Firestop Exam** an individual sits. IAS AC291 Table 1, category 21, accepts the UL and FM firestop exams as the minimum qualification for a firestop **special inspector**.
- **Wrong in:** Credential check row 33, brief claim 29
- **Source:** IAS AC291, effective 2024-01-01, Table 1

### 6. AWC does publish the course — partly CONTRADICTED
"Durable Construction with Preservative-Treated Wood" is a course on **AWC's own learning portal** (learn.awc.org). The workbook is right that it's a course, not a certification. It's wrong to suggest the issuer "is not AWC".
- **Wrong in:** Credential check row 34, Sources R26

### 7. ACI anchor programs are commentary, not code — OVERSTATED
ACI 318-19 **code** text 26.13.1.6 requires a certified inspector for adhesive anchors. 26.13.1.5 allows a certified inspector **or** a qualified inspector approved by the licensed design professional and building official for mechanical anchors. The code names no program. CPP 681.1 and 681.2 appear only in the **commentary** (R26.13.1.5 / R26.13.1.6), as examples followed by "or similar program". IAS AC291 accepts ICC Reinforced Concrete SI for post-installed anchors.
- **Overstated in:** Flags SI-016 ("CONFLICT"), Decision 9, Credential check row 17
- Harness check T-07 passes because it finds "681.2" somewhere in the extract. That text is commentary, so the check proves a string exists, not a requirement.

## Missed by the prior work

1. **IAS AC291 Table 1 was never consulted.** It's IAS's minimum certification table for special inspection agencies, and the matrix has an IAS column. It lines up with DPP's credentials almost row for row: cold-formed steel → ICC B1/B2; prestressed → ICC 92 + RC SI; smoke control → AABC/NEBB; firestop → UL/FM exam; soils → ICC EC or NICET II. U-05 says there is "no external referent" for the credential sets. That's not accurate: this is one.
2. **IBC 2024 §1705.3.1 itself requires AWS D1.4** for rebar welding inspection and inspector qualification. The workbook cites only ACI 318, but the base code says it directly. SI-014 and SI-018 are on firmer ground than stated.
3. **IBC 2024 §1705.2.3 names SDI QA/QC** as the basis for qualifying welding special inspectors on cold-formed steel deck. SI-010's credential basis reads "policy" and arguably should include "standard".
4. **NEW-04 (1705.11 Fabricated Items) isn't new.** It was 1705.10 in 2018. The five Fabricator rows (SI-003 to SI-007) already cover fabrication inspection. Calling it a "2024 section with no row" in Decision 5 misstates both halves.
5. **The SI FORM cites "ROH Chapter 16-2 Section R109.6".** ROH 16-2 is *Relocation of Buildings*. The residential special-inspection sections are in **ROH 16-1.2**, amend. (11). This is a pre-existing DPP error. Decision 13 caught the wrong subsection but not the wrong article.
6. **ROH 16-1.1 is amended through Ord. 25-11**, per the history note, not just 24-15. The adoption sentence is unchanged (still State code of 20 Apr 2021, which adopts the 2018 IBC), but the citation should say "as amended".
7. **IAS AC291 still lists NICET Geotechnical Engineering Technology** (N-I-GET to N-IV-GET) as an acceptable field qualification, in a document effective 2024. That doesn't prove NICET still issues it, but it bears directly on U-01. Also, the hosted NICET manual is labeled "Fourth Edition November 2012", not only June 1994.

## Stated more confidently than the evidence supports

- **Decision 6 / Flags SI-025** say the label "Special Grading, Excavation/Filling" is wrong. But ROH 1705.21 itself requires special inspection where conditions are "specified in ROH Chapter 18A", which is the grading chapter. The DPP label is defensible. Recommend softening to a question.
- **Decision 10** says the legacy ICC number "no longer identifies a single exam". True, but it still identifies the **certification**, which is what the matrix names. Restructuring the exams doesn't make the matrix wrong.
- **Sources R12** says ICC's catalog confirmed that "49, 1B, 2B and EC do NOT appear". Two of those four are wrong (49 and EC both appear; see fixes 1–2). B1/B2 *are* the current codes, so the 1B/2B finding stands. But anything else resting on that one catalog read should be re-checked.
